## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed for the **Malaysia Data Innovation Talent (MDIT) × Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM) Datathon**.
Our team designed an interactive Power BI dashboard analysing graduate labour market, employment, salary trends and workforce outcomes in Malaysia using official DOSM datasets.
The dashboard enables users to explore labour market indicators through interactive visualisations and supports evidence-based decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyse graduate employment and labour market trends across Malaysia.
- Compare graduate salary levels across different states.
- Visualise labour force participation and unemployment indicators.
- Develop an interactive dashboard to support data-driven decision-making.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Graduate salaries increased steadily across most Malaysian states between 2020 and 2023.
- Labour force participation rates varied significantly across states.
- Graduate unemployment rates remained higher in some regions despite overall labour market recovery.
- Interactive filters enable comparisons across years, states and employment indicators.
